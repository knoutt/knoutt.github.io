TK-05

Desktop Flight Radar

Embedded · Enclosure · Data

A palm-sized instrument that lives under my monitor and shows every aircraft over Toronto in real time.

The round touchscreen draws a city basemap with live traffic, each plane tagged with its callsign, type and altitude, plus the local temperature up top. Tap an aircraft and it opens a detail card with a photo of that exact airframe, the airline, route, altitude, ground speed, distance from my desk, track, bearing and climb rate.

TK-04

PC Volume Mixer

Hardware · Firmware · Desktop app

Physical faders for per-app volume, so Discord, Spotify and a game never have to fight over one slider.

Four faders each control a program, and the strip display mirrors every level with the app's icon and colour. The status LEDs under each fader match. Three keys page between fader banks, and a companion desktop app assigns any program to any fader and talks to the mixer over USB.

The fader caps are printed dice: count the pips for the channel number.

TK-03

Synchronized Three-Screen Photo Display

ESP32 · ESP-NOW

Three tiny screens in a scale model, flipping through photos in a staggered sequence. A Valentine's Day gift.

Each screen is driven by its own ESP32 with an SPI TFT and microSD card. A master unit runs the slideshow and broadcasts timing over ESP-NOW so the two followers stay in step.

Most of the work was in the details: a custom embedded image pipeline, plus chasing down SPI bus sharing, display orientation, colour correction and SD card reliability.

TK-02

FieldMesh Rapid-Deploy Mesh Hub

LoRa · Raspberry Pi

A self-contained mesh communications and sensing kit that works with no internet at all.

Mesh nodes are LilyGO T-Beam units (ESP32, LoRa, GPS) running Meshtastic, acting as both data sources and relays. A Raspberry Pi Zero 2 W hub connects to a T-Beam over USB and serves a local Wi-Fi dashboard that opens in any browser.

One FastAPI service on the Pi bridges the radio to the dashboard: it normalizes packets, keeps live node state in memory with optional SQLite history, and streams updates over SSE behind simple local auth.

LilyGO T-Beam, Meshtastic
Hub

Software

TK-01

O11 Vision Grommet Cover & RTX 5090 Shroud

Reverse engineering

Two printed parts that make my PC look the way I wanted, not the way it shipped.

The Lian-Li O11 Vision Compact has a large empty void above the motherboard. I reverse-engineered the original grommet cover and designed a sculpted replacement that fills the gap, ties into the existing geometry and hides the cable runs.

The GPU shipped with a loud, angular paint job and no lighting. I modeled a snug ABS shroud that masks the stock styling and integrates a channel for an LED strip.
