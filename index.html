Trackside

A desk mixer for per-app volume

A USB desk mixer that gives Windows programs physical faders. Four 60 mm faders, three keys and a 2.79" bar display sit beside the keyboard. Each fader controls one program (Discord, Spotify, a game, whatever's in front) or the master volume, and one key press switches the faders to another bank of programs.

Custom PCB, firmware and a desktop app, with about 3,800 automated tests behind them. The fader caps are dice, so the pips tell you which channel is which.

How it's built

Hardware

  • Custom PCB designed in KiCad, assembled by JLCPCB
  • ESP32-S3
  • 16-bit ADC for the faders
  • Low-profile hot-swap keys
  • Addressable RGB lighting
