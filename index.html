Trackside

A desk mixer for per-app volume

A USB desk mixer that gives Windows programs physical faders. Four 60 mm faders, three keys and a 2.79" bar display sit beside the keyboard. Each fader controls one program (Discord, Spotify, a game, whatever's in front) or the master volume, and one key press switches the faders to another bank of programs.

Custom PCB, firmware and a desktop app, with about 3,800 automated tests behind them. The fader caps are dice, so the pips tell you which channel is which.

How it's built

Hardware

  • Custom PCB designed in KiCad, assembled by JLCPCB
  • ESP32-S3
  • 16-bit ADC for the faders
  • Low-profile hot-swap keys
  • Addressable RGB lighting

Experience

Diversified Power Inc.

Special Project Lead

Diversified Power, Ormond and Diversified Lifting Solutions are sister companies in hydraulic tooling and lifting. My job there is different every day, which is most of the fun.

The big one was moving the company off a DOS-based system and onto Odoo. I ran it pretty much start to finish: sat down with everyone to see how they actually work, sorted out problems as they came up, tested everything, moved the data over and trained people one-on-one. Jobs that used to get passed around on paper now live in one system the whole team can see, nothing goes missing, and it opened the door to selling online. Before that, I moved our rentals from paper to dedicated rental software, and led our Canada Digital Adoption Program project.

Day to day I help keep things running. I run our inventory counts, from pulling the numbers to checking the results, and dig in when the stock values don't add up. I built a service request form for the website so customers give us the details we need up front, which means fewer mistakes, better leads and fewer phone calls. I also redid the websites for all three companies (Diversified Power, Ormond and Diversified Lifting Solutions), and Ormond's brought in more rentals.

And when customers need equipment, I help them figure out what fits, price it out, and go on site visits to show them the ordering and service portals we build.
